# Thai Herbal Registry

Thai herbs are represented at three distinct levels:

1. Botanical identity — Thai/English/scientific names and accepted taxonomy.
2. Material — plant part, powder/extract/essential oil, solvent, extraction method, and standardization marker.
3. Chemical constituent — a separately resolved compound linked to PubChem/RDKit/QSAR when a defined molecular structure exists.

A whole extract, essential oil, fragrance, or variable botanical mixture is never assigned the SMILES of one marker compound. Whole-material evidence is reported as botanical/literature evidence; QSAR applies only to structure-resolved constituents. Coverage must show known, resolved, QSAR-assessed, literature-only, and unresolved constituent counts.

Bulk ingestion requires documented licensing or permission. Source URL, retrieval time, raw-file hash, and evidence provenance must be retained.
